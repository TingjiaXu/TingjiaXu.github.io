Selected Applied Projects

01

Health-Oriented Community Public Space Micro-Regeneration Handbook

National Student Innovation Project · Advisors: Assoc. Prof. Yin Jie; Prof. Lan Wang

2022–2023

We developed a health-oriented framework for community micro-regeneration, organized around four health dimensions and translated into practical strategies for five spatial elements: streets, mobility, greening, squares, and buildings. The project produced two handbook versions: a full design handbook and a public-facing edition.

My role: Team lead; coordinated framework development and strategy synthesis; developed the street module and visuals; delivered the final presentation.
