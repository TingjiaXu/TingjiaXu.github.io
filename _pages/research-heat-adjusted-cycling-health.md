  <section class="research-detail-section research-detail-text research-abstract" aria-labelledby="research-overview-title">
    <h2 id="research-overview-title">Overview</h2>
    <p>
      This study examines how much of cycling’s health benefit remains after accounting for heat exposure. We developed the Heat-Exposure Adjusted
      Cycling Health Benefit Index (HCBI), which places cycling-related physical-activity benefits and heat-related health risks within a unified
      comparable framework. Using cycling trajectories, land-surface temperature, and built-environment data, HCBI was estimated at 100 m resolution
      across Shanghai. Machine-learning analyses were then used to examine how urban form shapes net health outcomes, revealing both trade-offs and
      nonlinear effects relevant to climate-resilient cycling and urban design.
    </p>
  </section>
