        <span class="project-number" aria-hidden="true">01</span>
        <div class="projects-entry-heading">
          <h3 id="handbook-project-title">Health-Oriented Community Public Space Micro-Regeneration Handbook</h3>
          <p class="projects-entry-meta">National Student Innovation Project · Advisors: Assoc. Prof. Yin Jie; Prof. Lan Wang</p>
        </div>
        <time datetime="2022/2023">2022–2023</time>
      </header>

      <div class="projects-entry-body">
        <p>
          We developed a health-oriented framework for community micro-regeneration, organized around
          <span class="projects-emphasis">four health dimensions</span> and translated into practical strategies for
          <span class="projects-emphasis">five spatial elements</span>: streets, mobility, greening, squares, and buildings. The project produced
          <span class="projects-emphasis">two handbook versions</span>: a full design handbook and a public-facing edition.
        </p>
        <p class="projects-role">
          <strong>My role:</strong> <span class="projects-emphasis">Team lead</span>; coordinated framework development and strategy synthesis; developed
          the street module and visuals; delivered the final presentation.
        </p>
      </div>
