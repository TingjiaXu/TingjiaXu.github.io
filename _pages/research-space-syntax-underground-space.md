  <section class="research-detail-section research-detail-text research-abstract" aria-labelledby="research-overview-title">
    <h2 id="research-overview-title">Overview</h2>
    <p>
      This project investigates how the underground public and commercial spaces around Shanghai's Jing'an Temple Metro Station can be evaluated and
      optimized using Space Syntax and data-driven analysis. Integration, Choice, and Visibility Graph Analysis were used to assess accessibility,
      spatial centrality, and visual accessibility, while pedestrian-flow surveys and agent-based simulations were used to examine actual and
      simulated movement patterns. The results were translated into spatial-design strategies aimed at improving pedestrian circulation, spatial
      legibility, user experience, and the placement of commercial functions.
    </p>
  </section>
